The Story

Born in the South.
Made for the Islands.

Chef Dre brought the soul of Southern cooking straight to the shores of Hawaii — slow-smoked meats, rich creamy sides, and flavors that hit different every single time. Every plate is cooked with intention, seasoned with love, and finished with that unmistakable Dre touch.

From hickory-smoked pulled pork sliders to bourbon-marinated jerk chicken, this is comfort food elevated — served fresh from the truck, right to your hands.
